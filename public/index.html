How it works

  1. Tap Start and get ready. You have 10 seconds to find your pose.
  2. Strike 4 poses. Watch the strip on the right to see how you fit in the frame.
  3. Want a redo? Tap Retake. You'll get the countdown again, and the second set will be saved or sent.
  4. Enter your email when your photo strip appears.
  5. Save your strip. Open your email, then on a phone, press and hold the photo and tap Save to Photos. On a computer, right-click it and choose Save image as.
